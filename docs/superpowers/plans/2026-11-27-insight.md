# Insight (Phase 3) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Charging insight, driving insight and a projected range on the existing Statistics and Battery pages.

**Architecture:** A new read-only `skoda_mate/analytics.py` computes all three from stored rows; `routers/reports.py` exposes
it; the Statistics page gains Charging and Driving tabs whose rendering lives in `static/lib/`, and the Battery page gains
one card. No schema change, no new dependency.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.15, FastAPI, SQLite, vanilla ES modules, pytest, puppeteer-core.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-places-insight-automation-design.md` §4.4, §4.5, §4.6, §7

## Global Constraints

- No new calls to Škoda: no analytics endpoint may call the poller or the budget.
- New endpoints take `ContextDep`, the existing session/bearer authorisation and `vehicle(ctx.conn, vid)`.
- Inferred and imported charges (`inferred`, `import-trip`) count toward energy and cost, never toward power or the curve.
- Unknown cost or energy stays unknown (`null`), never zero.
- A curve bin (5 % SoC) with fewer than 3 samples is not returned. AC and DC never mix.
- The annual projection appears only after 60 tracked days and is labelled a projection. No speed, elevation or routes.
- Range points need SoC >= 20, range > 0 and a projection within 200–1000 km. The copy never says "battery health" for it.
- Statistics tabs are `Overview`, `Charging`, `Driving`, `Parked`; the period control is shared.
- Every visible string is `t('English')` with a German entry; DOM via `h()`; no inline script; colours from CSS variables.

## Decisions the spec leaves open

- **Heatmaps** use measured times only: inferred/imported charges and `approx` trips have estimated start times, so they
  are left out and counted in `heatmap_excluded`.
- **Average power** is total energy / total hours over measured charges with both; `end_estimated` charges are left out.
- **Outlier filter:** "recent median" is the median of the readings within ±15 days of the point's day (a single global
  median would delete a whole winter). A point stays within 3 median absolute deviations; the deviation has a floor of 1 %
  of that median, so a flat series keeps its integer-rounding noise.
- **Trend:** least-squares slope of the daily medians in km per year, from 60 days of span and 10 days with data.
- **Degradation:** first and last 90-day windows, only when they do not overlap and each has at least 20 days with data.
- **Periods:** the 6/12-month choice starts on the first day of the month, so it lines up with the monthly table. Months
  are bucketed like the monthly table (UTC); `from`/`to` are local days (`local_bound`).

## Changes made while executing

- The projected range is kept until a reading is added or removed: on three years of history it took 0.8 s, and the
  Battery page asks every 30 s.
- Added after review: the Parked table is paged by the server (`limit`/`offset`, 50 a page, newest first) with totals
  over the whole period; `aggregates.flagged` replaces counting the session list on the Overview.
- Demo fix found by the longer history: a weekend errand under about 4.6 km arrived before its "in motion" reading.
- Demo evening charges at home are now always AC; fast charging happens at the Darmstadt stop.
- At 360 px, numbers in the new tables and the units in stat tiles broke across lines. The browser check now covers this.

## Review Focus

- A database with only inferred charges: energy and cost shown; no power, no curve, empty heatmap, no crash.
- A charge or trip with `energy_kwh`, `distance_km` or places missing: stays unknown / "Unknown place".
- A charge near local midnight: the heatmap uses the viewer's weekday and hour (`tz_offset`), including week wrap.
- A reversed or malformed `from`/`to`: a 400 with a plain message.
- A slow answer for a tab or period already left must not draw over the current one.
